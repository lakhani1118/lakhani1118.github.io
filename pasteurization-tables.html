Adapted Baldwin’s table has six fat columns. Each low-fat time here is the average of the 1, 3 and 5% columns, and each high-fat time the average of the 7, 9 and 12% columns.

Reading the tables

  • Hold times start once the center of the food reaches the bath temperature. They don’t include the time it takes to heat up.
  • “Instant” means the food is pasteurized as soon as the center reaches that temperature.
  • Times are shown in hours, minutes and seconds, converted from the original decimal minutes.
  • Chicken and turkey times are averaged into low fat (1–5%) and high fat (7–12%) columns.

Both tables are from Appendix C, “Government Pasteurization Tables”, of Douglas Baldwin’s A Practical Guide to Sous Vide Cooking (version 0.4k), which also covers heating times and the reasoning behind these numbers. The primary sources are cited under each table as Baldwin gives them.
